Explore PC components

Laptops for study and coding

A laptop combines a screen, keyboard, battery and computer in one portable device. Its suitability depends on the software you need to run, so check your course requirements before choosing one.

  • Processor: carries out instructions and affects how quickly tasks run.
  • RAM: helps when several applications or browser tabs are open.
  • Storage: provides space for the operating system, applications and files.
  • Screen and battery: affect comfort and portability.

Inside a desktop PC

Main computer components and their roles
ComponentWhat it does
CPUProcesses instructions and calculations.
MotherboardConnects components so they can communicate.
RAMTemporarily holds data for active tasks.
SSD or hard driveStores files and applications when the power is off.
GPUProcesses graphics for the display; it may be integrated or a separate card.
Power supplySupplies suitable electrical power to the components.
CoolingHelps remove heat from components.

Useful peripherals

A keyboard and mouse provide input, a monitor displays output, and a headset supports audio and calls. A webcam can help with online lessons, while an external drive can hold a backup of important files.
